Privacy

Data stays under your control

JichoBot has no telemetry. Agent events and local history are processed by the app on your device. Pairing sends the minimum data needed to establish a connection. The optional relay forwards push messages when configured; it is not enabled by default in this source tree.

Local logs may contain project paths or snippets supplied by an agent. Review them before sharing. Credentials belong in the operating system keystore or Keychain.
