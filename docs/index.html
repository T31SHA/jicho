A desktop companion for AI coding agents

Keep your agents in view.

JichoBot follows sessions from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, and more. See what each agent is doing, answer supported questions, and review permission requests without losing your place. Kito keeps the island company.

Free and open source · MIT license · macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone, and Android

A quieter way to keep up with agent work.

When agents work in parallel, JichoBot puts their activity and requests in one small place on your screen. Follow a session, see its latest tool or question, then open the terminal when you want to take over.

How it helps

The useful parts stay close.

01

See every session at a glance

Follow thinking, tool use, questions, errors, and finished tasks across your agents. The island can show the current tool, recent steps, subagent activity, and a short task summary.

02

Keep permission decisions yours

For agents with a decision hook, JichoBot brings the request forward so you can allow or deny it. If an agent does not support a JichoBot decision, its own prompt stays in charge.

03

Get back to the right context

Answer supported agent questions, inspect changes where available, send a follow-up, or jump back to the terminal. Local session history and recaps help you pick up where you left off.

Approval cards

Available for Claude Code, Codex, Claude Desktop, GitHub Copilot CLI, Muse Code, and OpenCode. Antigravity approvals are opt-in. Cursor approval cards are available on Windows and Linux. Gemini CLI, Hermes, Amp, and other agents keep approval in their own interface.

More of your workflow

A companion, not another command center.

Chat and context

Use supported AI providers, ask about your work, and bring files or screen captures into supported conversations. macOS includes additional voice, dictation, and capture tools.

Services you already use

Follow useful activity from GitHub, Vercel, Stripe, n8n, Resend, Notion, Cal.com, and other integrations. What is available depends on the app build and the credentials you configure.

Kito keeps the rhythm

Kito changes expression with agent activity, gives the island a recognizable face, and has a wardrobe of looks you can choose from.

Chat providers and service integrations vary by platform. Credentials stay in the operating system’s secure storage.

Take the companion with you

Your work, within reach.

Pair JichoBot with a phone to check activity and handle supported requests away from the desktop.

iPhone

See agent activity, questions, widgets, and supported Live Activities. Local pairing is available; remote push requires Apple signing, CloudKit, APNs, and a configured relay.

iPhone companion details →

Android

Pair over your local network to see status and handle supported approvals and questions. An optional relay can carry updates when your phone is away from home.

Open source. No telemetry.

Your work stays yours.

Agent activity and local history are handled on your device. JichoBot has no telemetry. The optional relay is not enabled by default; it is only needed for remote delivery. Review the privacy page for the details.
