01 · See the work

Every session, at a glance

Follow agent activity, current tools, questions, subagents, errors, and finished tasks from a compact island. Open a session when you want the full story.

02 · Stay in control

Review requests before they run

When an agent asks for permission, see the request beside your work and allow or deny it from the island. The agent’s own safety rules remain in charge.

03 · Keep moving

Ask, answer, and send context

Respond to agent questions, jump back to the right terminal, chat with supported AI providers, and bring files or screenshots into the conversation.

04 · Connect your tools

Your services, one glance away

Keep an eye on work across GitHub, Vercel, Stripe, n8n, Resend, Notion, Cal.com, and more. Connect the services you use and choose what appears.

Pair an iPhone or Android companion to check activity, handle supported requests, and see updates from your phone. Remote delivery is optional and needs setup.

JichoBot can follow sessions from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, GitHub Copilot CLI, Muse Code, OpenCode, Amp, Hermes, and Claude Desktop. Third-party hooks let other tools send activity too.

What you can approve depends on the agent and desktop build. Claude Code, Codex, Copilot CLI, Muse Code, OpenCode, Antigravity, and Cursor on Windows/Linux have approval flows. Some agents show activity while keeping approval in their own interface.

Your work stays yours

Private by default.
Open by design.

JichoBot has no telemetry. Agent activity and local history stay on your device; credentials belong in your operating system’s secure store. An optional relay is not enabled by default and is only needed for remote phone delivery.

A few useful details

Good to know.

Which computers can run JichoBot?

JichoBot includes desktop apps for macOS, Windows, and Linux, plus phone companions for iPhone and Android. Some integrations and approval flows are specific to a platform or app build.

Does JichoBot approve every agent request?

No. It shows activity for many agents, while approval cards depend on each agent’s hook support. If an agent does not support an approval decision through JichoBot, its own prompt remains in charge.

Can I use the phone app away from my computer?

Local pairing is the simple path. Remote push and delivery use an optional relay and require platform-specific setup; they are not switched on automatically.

Is JichoBot free?

JichoBot is free and open source under the MIT license. The optional relay can use your own service account and deployment.
